Expert Calls & Meetings: Get Real Industry Answers Fast

Learn how expert calls and meetings help businesses get real industry answers fast, prepare better questions, and turn expert insight into actionable decisions.

What Do We Mean by “Expert Calls & Meetings”?

An expert call is a scheduled one-on-one (or small group) conversation between a business decision-maker and an industry professional who has direct, current experience in a specific field. It could be a former hospital procurement manager explaining how medical devices actually get approved for purchase, a supply chain veteran walking through real bottlenecks, or a regional operator describing what is happening on the ground in a market that looks completely different on paper.

Unlike a survey or a published report, an expert meeting is a live, two-way conversation. You can ask follow-up questions, push back on an answer, and steer the discussion toward the exact detail that matters for your decision.

Key idea: An expert meeting lets you ask targeted questions and explore details that published information may not capture.

Why Expert Calls & Meetings Matter More Than Ever

A few forces have made expert conversations more important, even in an age of abundant data.

Current context can add depth to published research

Information moves fast. By the time a market report is published, the market may already have shifted.

Public data can be noisy. An experienced expert can add context around assumptions, numbers, and changing conditions.

Nuance does not fit in a spreadsheet. Buyer psychology, regulatory quirks, and industry dynamics often emerge through conversation.

The Usual Challenges of Setting Up Expert Meetings

  • Finding the right person. Narrow expertise can take time to source.
  • Vetting and compliance. Regulated industries require appropriate checks.
  • Scheduling friction. Time zones, calendars, and NDAs can slow things down.
  • Inconsistent quality. Without a structured process, a call may not produce useful insight.

What to Prepare Before an Expert Call

  • Write down your top 3 to 5 questions in advance.
  • Share context with the expert ahead of time.
  • Decide who needs to be in the room.
  • Leave room for follow-up.

Getting the Most Out of Every Expert Meeting

  • Record the call with permission so important details are not lost.
  • Ask what the expert would investigate if they were in your position.
  • Compare notes across multiple calls for important decisions.
  • Follow up with a specific question when useful.

Final Thoughts

Expert calls and meetings work because they bring direct, current human experience into the research process. That perspective can complement published research and other sources of evidence.
